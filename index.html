Currently

At MIT and UFSCar I am building QR-Ω, a communication-avoiding 2.5D Householder QR in C++/CUDA. On one H200 it is 1.4–2.2× faster than tuned cuSOLVER and up to 11.9× faster than MAGMA and SLATE below n = 32,000, and on eight H200 GPUs it is faster than cuSOLVERMp.

For MIT's CHEFSI center, a DOE/NNSA PSAAP-IV exascale simulation center, I develop distributed mixed-precision sparse solvers in Julia and PETSc that run 2–10× faster than the application team's solver.

I co-maintain Dagger.jl, an open-source Julia task runtime like Legion and PaRSEC. My FAPESP master's project at UFSCar, advised by Prof. Hermes Senger, recasts seismic wave propagation so that time stepping runs as batched mixed-precision GEMM and TRSM on GPU tensor cores.

Research areas

Dense factorizations on GPUs

Householder QR with tensor-core modes (TF32 and 3×TF32) and multi-GPU communication through NVSHMEM and NCCL.

Sparse linear solvers

Krylov methods, multigrid and Schwarz preconditioners, sparse direct solvers, and Float32 factors under Float64 refinement.

Distributed task runtimes

Dagger.jl: MPI one-sided communication, multi-GPU arrays on NVIDIA, AMD and Apple GPUs, and data-dependency scheduling.

Wave propagation

Acoustic frozen-wave beams (JASA 2026) and tensor-core time stepping for seismic imaging (RTM, FWI).
