MIT and UFSCar | 2026 – Present

QR-Ω: communication-avoiding 2.5D Householder QR on GPUs

  • Designed and implemented a C++/CUDA Householder QR 1.4–2.2× faster than tuned cuSOLVER and up to 11.9× faster than MAGMA and SLATE below n = 32,000 on one NVIDIA H200; at large n it reaches 82–88% of GEMM throughput and matches cuSOLVER with 60% of its residual.
  • Tall-skinny matrices: up to 3.3× faster than cuSOLVER, 83× faster than MAGMA and SLATE.
  • Added tensor-core modes that reach 249 TFLOP/s in TF32 (5.3× the fastest FP32 library) and 94 TFLOP/s in 3×TF32, which keeps FP32-class accuracy at 2.0× the speed.
  • Extended it to eight H200 GPUs, where it is the fastest library at every size and precision tested: 1.02–1.12× ahead of cuSOLVERMp and up to 1,327 TFLOP/s (TF32, n = 327,680).
  • Stack: C++/CUDA, CUTLASS/CuTe kernels, cuBLASLt, NVSHMEM, NCCL, Nsight Compute profiling.

MIT's DOE/NNSA PSAAP-IV exascale simulation center | Oct 2025 – Present

Sparse linear solvers for CHEFSI

  • Built a C++17 PETSc benchmark suite sweeping Krylov solvers and preconditioners on CPU and GPU (cuSPARSE, cuSOLVER): sparse direct (MUMPS, SuperLU_DIST, STRUMPACK), multigrid (GAMG, BoomerAMG) and additive Schwarz; tuned GAMG and ASM with Optuna (Python).
  • Develop distributed mixed-precision sparse solvers (Julia, PETSc/C) with SmartSolve.jl's LLM proposer-refiner agents, 2–10× faster than the application team's solver: Schwarz and incomplete-Cholesky preconditioned CG, Float32 factors under Float64 refinement, MPI SpMV.

FAPESP master's project, UFSCar | Aug 2025 – Present

Wave propagation on GPU tensor cores

  • Recast finite-difference seismic wave propagation as superstep matrix operators (exponential integration of the sparse spatial operator), compressed by selected Schur decomposition and Balanced POD so time stepping runs as batched mixed-precision GEMM/TRSM on tensor cores.
  • Build the reduced bases with Krylov methods (GMRES, rational Krylov, shift-invert) on structured sparse operators; target 2D/3D acoustic benchmarks for seismic imaging (RTM, FWI).
  • Stack: C++/CUDA (cuBLAS, WMMA tensor cores), MPI, CMake, GoogleTest, Git, Linux.

MIT Julia Lab and Google Summer of Code | 2023 – Present

Dagger.jl

  • Co-maintain Dagger.jl, a Julia task runtime like Legion and PaRSEC: 100+ releases, 60+ contributors, 730+ GitHub stars; used at CERN, CESMIX, Barcelona Supercomputing Center and INPE.
  • Implemented the MPI backend (non-blocking and one-sided RMA communication) and scaled runs to 500+ nodes on Argonne's Aurora supercomputer.
  • Designed the multi-GPU array API (map-reduce, core linear algebra) for NVIDIA (CUDA), AMD (ROCm) and Apple GPUs; ran Cholesky on 256 AMD GPU nodes of LLNL's Tuolumne.
  • Added OpenMP-style data-dependency annotations (Datadeps), published at IEEE HPEC 2024.
  • As a 2023 Google Summer of Code contributor, built MPI-backed distributed arrays (distributed QR, LU and Cholesky) and finished the move from lazy to eager execution, making array operations up to 60% faster.
  • Co-mentored two Google Summer of Code contributors (2025, 2026), both presenting ACM SRC posters at SC25 and SC26.
